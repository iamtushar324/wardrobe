# Notices

Wardrobe is written from scratch. Ideas were borrowed (no code) from these projects:

- [wardrowbe](https://github.com/Anyesh/wardrowbe) — self-hosted wardrobe with AI tagging and outfit suggestions.
- [tandpfun/wardrobe](https://github.com/tandpfun/wardrobe) — a clean, photo-first closet gallery.
- [Libre-Closet](https://github.com/lazztech/Libre-Closet) — open closet organiser.

Background removal uses the **IS-Net (DIS)** general-use model in the ONNX build published by
[rembg](https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg) (Apache-2.0). It is downloaded at first use into the data
directory and is not part of this repository.

AI pictures are made by the image models of the services you connect (ChatGPT through the Codex CLI, or
OpenRouter). Their terms apply to what you generate.
